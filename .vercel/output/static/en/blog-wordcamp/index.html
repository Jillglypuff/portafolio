Dear Diary, My Experience at WordCamp 2026: Community, Code, and Connections 🌸

A developer diary by Jill

There are events you attend expecting to hear about tech... and you end up leaving with something far more meaningful.

That was exactly my experience at WordCamp WordPress 2026.

Attending this edition of WordCamp reminded me why the open source community is so special.

It wasn't just about slides, code snippets, or tools. It was about the people who make this ecosystem thrive.

I had the incredible opportunity to listen to inspiring talks and, above all, to connect with developers, creators, and enthusiasts who share a genuine passion for the web.

Keynotes & Industry Leaders: Carlos Longarela & Matías Ventura

One of the biggest highlights of the event was having a front-row seat to listen to two key leaders in the WordPress universe: 🌸

• Matías Ventura — Creator and Lead Architect of the Gutenberg block editor. Listening to his vision on modern content editing, block architecture, and the future of Wordpress was truly inspiring! 🌷

• Carlos Longarela — Certified Expert WordPress Developer, WordPress employee, and active content creator who builds popular plugins and technical resources for the community. His talk was an eye-opener on the Hermes Agent and the power of running local AI models on your own machine! He explained how local AI models protect privacy, guarantee data security, and keep sensitive client data safe without sending it off to third-party cloud models.

The True Highlight: Human Connections

Beyond the tech sessions, what completely stole my heart was the warmth and energy during the breaks! 🌸💖

Some attendees approached me to talk about my experience as a Full Stack developer: asking about project architecture, my tech stack, and how I am able to manage everything as a full-stack developer (spoiler: a healthy mix of curiosity, passion, and occasionally arguing with CSS flexbox alignment! 😂).

Another highlight was reconnecting with friends and attendees I had met at last year's WordCamp! It was so heartwarming to greet them again, and I was so happy that they remembered me — we caught up, talked about what we've been building since, and shared a moment together. 🌷

What touched me deeply was that people older than me, with extensive experience in the industry, approached me with genuine curiosity and respect to listen to my perspective and ask for tips and advice.

It reminded me that tech isn't about competition or egos. It's about curiosity, building together, and lifting each other up across every generation! 🌷

Technical Notes

Key technical takeaways and takeaways gathered during WordCamp 2026. 🌸

1. The Future of WordPress: AI Agents, MCPs & Gutenberg

Matías Ventura's vision for upcoming WordPress updates & AI integration:

// Model Context Protocol (MCP) & AI Agents in WordPress
import { registerBlockType } from '@wordpress/blocks';

// Gutenberg evolving towards agentic workflows & structured context
console.log('WordPress + MCP + Agentic Workflows ✨');

WordPress is transforming into an intelligent component ecosystem where AI agents and MCPs assist content creators and developers directly inside the block editor.

2. Hermes Agent & Local AI Models for Maximum Privacy

Carlos Longarela's privacy-first local AI architecture:

Sensitive Local Data → Local LLM / Hermes Agent → Private Response ✨
          [ ✖ NO External Data Exposure / NO Cloud Risk ]

Carlos Longarela highlighted how Certified Expert WordPress Developers can leverage tools like Hermes Agent and local AI models to keep client data 100% private and secure locally.

Things I Want To Remember

  • ✦WordCamp 2026 was a sweet reminder that behind every line of code are wonderful human connections. 🌸
  • ✦Matías Ventura's inspiring session on Gutenberg's future, upcoming updates, AI agents, and MCPs.
  • ✦Carlos Longarela's talk as a Certified Expert WordPress Developer on Hermes Agent & local AI security.
  • ✦Reconnecting with friends from last year's WordCamp and making new meaningful connections. 🌷
  • ✦Older and highly experienced developers approaching me to ask for advice and share ideas. 💖
  • ✦Curiosity and learning have no age limit — software is a shared, beautiful journey.
